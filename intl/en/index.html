Frequently asked questions

Do you see children from overseas?

Yes. Please send an inquiry first so we can review your child's information and plan the visit.

Is the remote review a diagnosis?

No. It is an information review to advise whether a visit is advisable. Diagnosis and treatment decisions are made after an in-person examination.

How long should we stay in Korea?

It depends on the tests needed. We estimate the number of days after reviewing your records.

Can we continue after returning home?

Yes. We arrange periodic video-call follow-up and offer a multilingual care app. Details are agreed individually.

Send an inquiry
